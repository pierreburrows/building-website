## Part 1: Banner

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Where | Higgsfield → Image → **Nano Banana Pro** (or Nano Banana 2.1 for the extra-wide formats) |
| Reference upload | Kravo "K" logo (black square, white K, blue slash) |
| Aspect ratio | 21:9 at 4K (or 8:1 on Nano Banana 2.1), then crop to **1128 × 191 px** for LinkedIn |
| Safe zone | Keep the bottom-left ~25% empty (the logo sits there on desktop). Keep all text centre-right (mobile crops the edges). |

### Prompt (copy-paste)

```
Ultra-wide website banner for a UK digital agency called Kravo Digital, which builds websites and CRMs with AI automation for small businesses.

Style: clean, premium, modern tech. Solid near-black background (#0B0B0F) with a subtle dark grain texture. Brand colours only: black, white and one electric blue accent (#2F6BFF) matching the uploaded K logo's blue slash. No other colours.

Composition, left to right:
- Far left third: empty dark space with a faint blue diagonal light streak echoing the slash in the logo. No text, no objects here.
- Centre: three floating, slightly angled glass UI cards connected by thin glowing blue lines, showing a flow:
  1) a phone showing a sleek website with a "Get a quote" button,
  2) a small AI chat bubble saying "New enquiry captured",
  3) a CRM pipeline board with columns labelled "New", "Quoted", "Won".
- Right side: headline text in a bold geometric sans-serif, white:
  "Websites. CRMs. AI that follows up."
  Underneath in smaller blue text: "kravio.uk"

Spelling must be exact. Flat, sharp, minimal, lots of negative space, soft blue glow, no people, no stock-photo look, no extra words, no watermark.
```

### If the text comes out wrong

Generate it again **without text** (delete the headline lines and add "no text anywhere"), then add the headline yourself in Canva or Figma:

- Headline: `Websites. CRMs. AI that follows up.` (white, bold, ~40 px at 1128 px width)
- Sub: `kravio.uk` (blue `#2F6BFF`)

### Backup prompt (simpler, text-free)

```
Ultra-wide minimal tech banner, near-black background, a single electric blue (#2F6BFF) diagonal light beam crossing from left to right, faint grid lines, three small glowing nodes connected by a thin blue line on the right half, lots of empty space, premium, no text, no people.
```

### Upload checklist

1. Open the company page as an admin: https://www.linkedin.com/company/kravodigital/home/?viewAsMember=true
2. Edit page → Cover image → upload the 1128 × 191 px crop.
3. Check the preview on desktop **and** mobile before saving. Nothing important should sit under the logo or at the far edges.
